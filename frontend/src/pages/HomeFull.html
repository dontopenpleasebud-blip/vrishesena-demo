Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about donating to Vrishasena Foundation

What is Vrishasena Foundation?
Vrishasena Foundation is a registered, Chennai-based non-profit NGO working across India to alleviate hunger and uplift communities through food, education, healthcare, and livelihood programmes. We have served over 115 lakh meals and worked with 6.5 lakh+ donors to create lasting change.
Why is Vrishasena Foundation the best NGO to donate to?
Vrishasena Foundation is trusted as one of the best NGOs because we operate with 100% transparency and ensure fast, on-time delivery of food and aid to those who need it most. Every donation is backed with photo and video proof and donor name recognition, and as an 80G-certified organisation we make sure your contribution reaches the right hands quickly and creates real, lasting impact.
Donate Now
Why should I donate to Vrishasena Foundation?
Vrishasena Foundation operates with 100% transparency, ensuring that every donation directly supports those in need. Our mission is to alleviate hunger and uplift communities through food, education, and healthcare, focusing on long-term solutions that create lasting change. We provide regular updates, allowing donors to see the tangible impact of their contributions. Together, we can transform lives and build a brighter future.
Donate Now
How is my donation used?
Every contribution goes directly toward our food, education, healthcare, and livelihood programmes. We maintain complete transparency and back each donation with photo and video documentation, along with donor name recognition, so you can see the real-world impact of your generosity.
Are donations to Vrishasena Foundation eligible for tax exemption?
Yes. Vrishasena Foundation is 80G certified, so your donations are eligible for tax exemption under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act. You will receive an official receipt that you can use to claim your tax deduction.
What causes can I support through Vrishasena Foundation?
You can donate to a wide range of causes including feeding the homeless, birthday celebrations for underprivileged children, education, healthcare, orphanage support, animal welfare, environmental initiatives, and livelihood programmes.
Donate Now
How can I see the impact of my donation?
For eligible donations you receive photo proof, a video of the celebration or distribution, and regular updates. This is part of our commitment to 100% transparency, so every donor can witness the difference they make.
How can I volunteer or get involved with Vrishasena Foundation?
You can join us as a volunteer to help serve meals, support children's education, assist young hospital patients, distribute essentials, and take part in tree-planting drives. Visit our Volunteer page to get started and make a difference today.
